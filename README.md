# Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn data from a telecommunications company to identify patterns, trends, and factors affecting customer retention.

The objective is to perform data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and customer segmentation to generate actionable business insights.

## Tasks Completed

* Dataset Understanding
* Data Cleaning
* Exploratory Data Analysis
* Customer Segmentation Visualization
* Business Insights & Recommendations

## Key Insights

* Customers with month-to-month contracts showed higher churn rates.
* Customers with shorter tenure were more likely to leave.
* Higher monthly charges correlated with increased churn probability.
* Long-term customers had better retention rates.

## Visualizations Included

* Churn Distribution
* Histograms
* Boxplots
* Correlation Heatmap
* Customer Segmentation Charts
* Contract Type Analysis

## Business Recommendations

* Improve retention strategies for new customers.
* Offer incentives for long-term contracts.
* Reduce churn through personalized customer engagement.
* Monitor high monthly charge customers closely.
